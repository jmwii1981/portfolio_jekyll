Heartland One was an initiative to break down the company’s siloed culture and encourage collaboration across teams.

Our team saw how that siloed culture affected our work. We were working in silos, making it difficult to collaborate across products and create consistent experiences that could scale. That separation stood in the way of us releasing excellent products. We were rebuilding the same components over and over again, experiences varied across each of our products, and accessibility clearly needed more attention.

I saw a shared design system as a way to address those challenges while supporting Heartland One’s broader goal. That work became Vega.

To put that vision into motion, I built the initial wireframing kit, explored the visual direction and built support with leadership and peers. As more people joined to shape it, my role expanded into facilitation, roadmaps and product ownership. Together, we developed a shared foundation that helped teams create consistent, accessible and higher quality experiences for our customers.

Vega grew beyond its beginnings at Heartland into a shared system adopted across Global Payments.
